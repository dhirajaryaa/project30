## What I did

Completed the journey timeline view — a grid of 30 cells showing completed/partial/missed/not-started states. Each cell links to the day entry. The timeline uses the same status marks (✓, ◐, —) across the entire app for consistency.

Built the share card page. When a day is shared, it renders a clean card with the day number, status, task, area, and username. Copy link and native Web Share API are both supported.

## What I learned

Consistency in visual language matters more than creativity in a system like this. The same status mark appearing in the timeline, the day page, the share card, and the OG image creates a coherent experience without any extra explanation.
