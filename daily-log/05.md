## What I did

Finished the day entry page. Day N / 30 header, task as the main heading, status with the ✓ mark, activity type, and formatted date in the meta row. The markdown body renders as proper journal sections with generous whitespace.

The page is clean enough that each section disappears and only the prose is left — exactly what a journal should feel like.

## What I learned

Typographic hierarchy does the heavy lifting. One 3xl heading, muted meta labels, and 8px line-height on 18px body text make a simple page feel like it was designed. No cards, no borders, no decoration — just hierarchy.
